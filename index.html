About

I'm Yash. I trained as an engineer, taught coding, and ghostwrote LinkedIn posts for founders at a personal-branding agency. Now I build sales pipelines for local businesses, along with my own tools to see where their customers are searching.

Your customers don't use one platform. They search Google, check Maps and your reviews, ask ChatGPT, open your website, then message you. So I build the whole path as one loop: found first, small ads only once organic shows what works, a CRM so no enquiry goes cold, and every sale traced back to the platform that brought it.

Because every sale is traced, I can work out where your next spend should go. When something works, you know why, and when it doesn't, you know where it broke.

The system still misses things sometimes. On one project, I was two months in before I noticed that nobody was tracking walk-in sales at the counter. I fixed it the way I fix most gaps: I built the missing piece.

Based in India. Working with clients across India and internationally.

Who this works for

This will feel right if

  • You already spend on marketing, but you can't point to which platform brought in which customer
  • Enquiries come in every week, and nobody can tell you where they came from
  • You'd rather see your real numbers, even the bad ones, than hear that everything is going well
  • You'd rather fix the tracking once than keep paying for ads you can't measure
  • You want a report you can check yourself, line by line

This won't be a fit if

  • You want ads running this week, before the basics are in place. Every result is tracked from day one.
  • You need results overnight. The first month goes into groundwork that everything after it depends on.
  • Price is the main deciding factor. I'm rarely the cheapest option, and I don't try to be.
  • You'd rather not share access to your ad accounts. I can't measure what I can't see.
