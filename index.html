About

Most marketers start with the ads. I start with the setup.

I come from an engineering background. That means I think about marketing like a system where every part is connected and every number is accounted for. Before running a single ad, I make sure we can track everything that matters.

Then I run campaigns on top of that so when something works, you know why. And when it doesn't, you know where it broke.

Based in Pune. Working with clients across India and internationally.

Is this a fit?

I work best with

  • Businesses that spend on marketing but can't see what's working
  • Companies with leads coming in but no system to track where they came from or follow up
  • Owners and founders who want to understand their own numbers, not just hand them off
  • Businesses ready to fix the tracking foundation before spending more on ads
  • Teams that want marketing they can measure, not "trust me, it's working"

Not the right fit if

  • You just want someone to run ads without any tracking setup
  • You need results overnight with no groundwork
  • You're looking for the cheapest option, not the right one
  • You're not comfortable sharing access to your ad accounts
